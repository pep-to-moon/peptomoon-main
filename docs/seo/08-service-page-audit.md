**Shared findings:** every page has a lead of 40 to 77 words, 6 to 10 FAQs, a process section, a "what we do not do" section, a related-services list and a single call to action (`/contact`). Trust signals are limited to the partner-built store references and the stated limits; **no page names a person, entity or evidence of results** (doc 19). Twelve pages cite no source. All use the same 17-H2 skeleton (C-01). No page has a table or a visible short answer block; guides do.

The repository sources mean this material came from the partner's code; use of client names, screenshots and repository-derived details needs written authorisation I cannot verify (you have said owners agree to be featured) |
| Search value | Case studies target brand-name queries (low volume); their SEO value is credibility, internal links and AI-citable detail, not ranking |

**To complete a case study without inventing anything, collect from each owner (in writing):** (1) the problem before the build, (2) why a custom build, (3) the key requirements and constraints, (4) three design decisions and their trade-offs, (5) the hardest technical or regulatory challenge, (6) outcomes with a number, date and source they can show you (for example product count, page-speed result, uptime, order handling time), only with permission, (7) what Peptomoon did versus the partner, (8) written authorisation to name the store and show screenshots.

The figures are genuine, the period and methodology line are on the page, and the importer never publishes queries or pages. **Three presentation problems remain:**

1. **Attribution.** The heading reads "What one of our stores reaches in Google" and the intro "an RUO store in our portfolio", while the case-studies page says the stores were "built by our partner". "Our stores" implies Peptomoon owns or ran the store's search work. This is a partner-built store's performance, not Peptomoon's own (C-03).
2. **Anonymous and unverifiable.** The store is "Store 1" with no name or link, so no one can check it. Anonymity is respectable, but anonymous proof is weak proof; name the store with the owner's permission or explain why it is anonymous.
3. **The trend is down.** The first four weeks (clicks 3,702, impressions 152,565) versus the last four full weeks (2,948 and 131,577) are down about 20 percent in clicks. It is honestly shown; it should not be the lead evidence of SEO skill. The audience is also consumer-shaped (80.5 percent mobile).

**Better presentation:** retitle to "Search Console figures from one partner-built RUO store", state the period, export date and "anonymised at the owner's request", say what Peptomoon's role was (build and technical setup, not ongoing SEO, if that is true), and plan to replace it with Peptomoon's own Search Console data once there is some. Never present these figures as Peptomoon's own organic performance.
