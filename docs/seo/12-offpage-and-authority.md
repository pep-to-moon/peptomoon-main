# 12. Off-page SEO and authority

Researched 19 November 2026 with free resources only. **No backlink counts appear for Peptomoon because the free tools I can reach do not provide them.** Ahrefs' free endpoint returns only Domain Rating, and Site Explorer and Brand Radar require a paid plan. Bing Webmaster Tools and Ahrefs Webmaster Tools both give own-site backlink reports free once the site is verified: that is the first off-page task.

**Is there enough external recognition to compete?** Not yet, and for payments and fulfilment terms Peptomoon cannot catch DR 40 to 70 incumbents quickly. It can compete sooner where competitors are weak (peptide marketing agencies and website builders, formation and white-label queries). Domain Rating is a relative link measure, not a ranking factor Google uses; the target is relevant, genuine links, not a number.

## 2. Realistic authority strategy (in order of effort and value)

| # | Tactic | Why it is legitimate and relevant | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Partner-store links** | Six featured stores; owners have agreed to be featured. A "built with" credit or a case-study mention is a natural editorial link between businesses that actually worked together | Ask each owner for a credit line or case-study link on their site. Do not exchange links in bulk or use hidden footers |
| 2 | **Own-site backlink visibility** | Free and required for any plan | Verify Bing Webmaster Tools and Ahrefs Webmaster Tools, record the baseline |
| 3 | **Relevant B2B directory profile** | Velixa Labs' own domain is DR 0.0 yet surfaces through Clutch (DR 91). Directories carry authority that new agencies lack | Create a Clutch (or equivalent) profile **only** with accurate details; collect real client reviews with permission. Never buy or write reviews |
| 4 | **Named expert, real profiles** | Entity signals: a LinkedIn company page and an accountable person's profile feed `sameAs` and E-E-A-T | After the owner decision in doc 19 |
| 5 | **Expert collaboration** | A payments or regulatory attorney who reviews the sensitive guides can be credited and may link to or cite the work. The law-firm content that ranks supports Peptomoon's stance | Offer review credit and a link to the attorney's practice if they agree |
| 6 | **Original, citable resource** | Journalists and bloggers link to data and checklists, not service pages | A downloadable supplier-documentation scorecard and the processor scorecard (doc 11). Anonymised partner data with written permission later |
| 7 | **Community participation** | Autocomplete shows "reddit" attached to payments, dropshipping and "start a business" phrases, so operators ask questions there | Answer questions helpfully from a real, named account within each community's rules. No link dropping, no sock puppets |
| 8 | **Relevant industry publications** | Payments and e-commerce trade press cover high-risk merchant topics | Contribute an expert comment or a short sourced article only when there is something new to say |

## 3. What not to do

Paid links or link networks; private blog networks; bulk or irrelevant directories; automated outreach; comment and forum spam; fake or purchased reviews; guest-post farms; "mentions" bought from AI-visibility vendors (Google lists inauthentic mentions as unhelpful, doc 02); cross-linking the six portfolio sites to push authority.
