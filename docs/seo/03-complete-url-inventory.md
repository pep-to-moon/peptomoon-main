**How to read the link column:** "in total" counts distinct pages that link to the URL anywhere (header, footer and mega menu included). ## Findings from the inventory

1. **No orphan pages.** Every sitemap URL is linked from at least one other page (lowest is 2 in body text).
2. **Two pages have zero body links:** `/why-peptomoon` and `/glossary` depend on the header and footer only. The glossary has 22 anchored terms that no guide links to.
3. **No duplicate titles, descriptions or H1s** across the 38 URLs.
4. **No sitemap URL lacks value for a reason that justifies removal.** The five legal pages carry little SEO value but must exist and cost nothing; keep them indexable.
5. **The weakest body-link counts** are `/services/telehealth-platform-development` (3), `/resources/wholesale-vs-dropshipping-vs-3pl-for-ruo-brands` (3) and `/services/vial-label-design` (4).
6. **Keyword reality check:** of the 36 non-legal URLs, the primary keyword has any Google autocomplete footprint for only some (see doc 06). Do not read the Primary keyword column as proof of demand.
