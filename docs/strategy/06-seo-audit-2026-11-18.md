## 1. Executive summary

1. **Biggest problem: Google has not been introduced to the site.** The domain has only an SPF TXT record. There is no `google-site-verification` TXT and no verification meta tag on any page, so Search Console is almost certainly not set up, the sitemap has not been submitted through it, and there is no indexing data. Ahrefs reports Domain Rating 0.0 and a web-search proxy found no peptomoon.com pages. Until this is done, nothing below can be measured.
2. **Biggest opportunity: the niche SERPs are weak.** The target queries return Fiverr gigs, high-risk processor sales pages and throwaway `.pages.dev` or `.vercel.app` sites. Peptomoon already has 15 services, 6 published sourced guides of roughly 2,700 to 3,100 words each, FAQs on every page, a glossary and real Search Console data from a partner store. The shutdown-recovery and supplier-documentation guides are the best fits once indexed.
3. **What changed since the last audit:** there was no earlier audit, so this is the baseline. Against strategy doc 04, nearly every planned technical item is live and verified: self-canonicals, 308 redirects to one host, real 404s with noindex, sitemap, robots, breadcrumbs, schema, per-route OG images, llms.txt. The gaps are measurement (GSC), trust signals (no named people or entity) and source depth.
4. **Biggest risk in what is about to ship:** the new build is slower to paint than live. Lab LCP is 4.8 s on /case-studies, 4.1 s on /all-in-one and 4.0 s on /why-peptomoon, against about 1.5 s on the current live pages. The cause is JavaScript-gated reveal animations and a lazy-loaded hero image (finding TECH-002). Fix this before deploying the 104-file change.
5. **Realistic target (a goal, not a promise):** by day 30 after verification, every sitemap URL is Indexed or has a stated reason in GSC and the brand query ranks first. By day 90, at least five non-brand queries appear in the top 20 and one query per core cluster has impressions. Reset these numbers after the first 28 days of real data.

## 3. Findings

### Crawl and indexation

**IDX-001 (VERIFIED) Search Console and Bing are not confirmed.**
- `curl -s "https://dns.google/resolve?name=peptomoon.com&type=TXT"` returns only `v=spf1 include:_spf.mail.hostinger.com ~all`.
- `curl -s https://peptomoon.com/ | grep -c google-site-verification` returns 0. `NEXT_PUBLIC_GSC_VERIFICATION` is empty in `.env.local` (length 0).
- Consequence: no sitemap submission, no URL Inspection, no query or coverage data, no way to request indexing.

**Crawl surface (VERIFIED, all pass).**
- robots.txt: `Allow: /`, `Disallow: /api/`, sitemap declared. 42 named AI, search and social crawlers share one explicit allow group, so they do not depend on the `*` group. Googlebot is not named and falls under `*`, which allows everything.
- Sitemap: 36 live URLs (38 local), all return 200, none are noindex, none redirect.
- `X-Robots-Tag` header: absent on all 36 pages. Every page carries `index, follow` with `max-snippet:-1` and `max-image-preview:large`.
- Canonicals: self-referencing on all pages, matching `og:url`, no trailing slash.
- Variants: `http://` and `https://www.` go to `https://peptomoon.com/` in one 308. `/services/` goes to `/services` in one 308. Uppercase paths and `/index.html` return 404. `?utm_source=` returns 200 and the canonical stays clean.
- Unknown URLs return a real 404 with `noindex` (`/nonexistent-page-xyz`).
- 59 unique internal links on the local build and 55 on live: all return 200. No generic anchors ("click here"). No orphan pages (lowest inbound count is 3 distinct pages).
- All content is in server HTML: the live homepage is 473 KB raw and 46 KB brotli.

**TECH-007 (VERIFIED, P3) Two-hop chain for `http://www.`.** `curl -sI http://www.peptomoon.com/services/payment-processing` returns `Location: https://www.peptomoon.com/services/payment-processing`, which then redirects to the bare domain. Harmless to Google, but a one-hop rule in Vercel is cleaner.

**TECH-004 (VERIFIED, P2) Sitemap dates carry no signal.** All 14 static pages, 13 services and 3 case studies share one constant (`SITE_UPDATED` in `src/lib/routes.ts`, 2026-11-17 live, 2026-11-18 local). Google ignores `priority` and `changefreq`, and discounts `lastmod` when it is not reliably accurate. Harmless today, but the dates cannot help later.

**IndexNow (VERIFIED absent, P3).** No key file. It only helps Bing and Yandex, not Google.

**TECH-002 (VERIFIED in lab, no field data yet).** First paint on /all-in-one is 1.25 s but LCP is 4.06 s. Causes found in the code and markup:
- `globals.css` lines 183 to 192: under the `.js` class, `[data-reveal]:not(.is-in)` is `opacity: 0` until JavaScript adds `.is-in`, so any text or image inside `Reveal` waits for hydration. `src/components/all-in-one/hero.tsx` wraps the hero lead paragraph in `<Reveal delay={1}>`.
- The /why-peptomoon hero image is rendered with `loading="lazy"` and no priority. No hero image on the four pages checked (/why-peptomoon, /case-studies, /services, /all-in-one) sets `fetchpriority="high"`, so the lazy hero image is the likely LCP element there. `Photo` in `src/components/ui/photo.tsx` supports a `priority` prop but it is not used on these heroes.
- The homepage DOM grew from 2,446 nodes (live) to 4,555 nodes (local build).
- Live is fine today, so this is a regression to catch before deploy. The skill rule is not to create CWV work while field data is green; here there is no field data and the lab result fails, on the pages most likely to be landing pages.

### E-E-A-T and trust

- **EEAT-001 (VERIFIED) No accountable human or entity.** A text scan of all 38 pages found no founder, team member, author or reviewer names and no registered company, office address or phone number (the site config itself notes there is no public phone yet). The editorial policy says guides are "written by the Peptomoon team and published under the team's name". For payments and regulatory topics (the strictest quality bar) this is the largest trust gap. It cannot be fixed by me: it needs facts from the owner and must never be invented.
- **EEAT-002 (VERIFIED) Thin primary sourcing.** Guides with 0 sources: `infrastructure-behind-an-ruo-peptide-business` (the pillar), `review-a-peptide-suppliers-documentation`, `peptide-store-architecture`. Others cite 1 to 2 (stripe.com, fda.gov, ecfr.gov, law.cornell.edu). 12 of 15 services cite none. The /all-in-one comparison table gives "commonly 3.5% to 6.5%" and "commonly 5% to 15%, held 3 to 6 months" labelled only as "industry estimates".
- Positives: editorial policy, correction policy, dated sources, disclaimer, privacy and terms, honest "what we do not do" sections.

### RUO compliance (scanned all titles, descriptions, H1s and body text)

- No dosing, injection, weight-loss, anti-aging, "buy" or human-use framing in any title, description or H1. The body hits for "dosing" and "human use" that I read in context (about, vial label design, customer support, telehealth, affiliate) are all negations ("we do not write dosing or human-use text"), and `pnpm check:content` already guards risky phrases.
- **COMP-001 (VERIFIED) The /all-in-one description states "from 3% with no rolling reserve" without a qualifier.** The page itself carries the qualifier (`paymentsOfferQualifier` in `src/content/all-in-one.ts`), and `/services/payment-processing` says "Honest about underwriting, no promises". The snippet is what searchers, payment partners and ad reviewers see first.
- **COMP-002 (NEEDS owner and counsel decision):** /all-in-one lists "Reconstitution and concentration calculators" three times as a deliverable. Reconstitution tools are commonly read as personal-use dosing aids, which sits against the site's repeated "we do not write dosing, usage or outcome content". Also, the title "All-in-One Peptide & Telehealth Business Partner" puts RUO products and a clinical offering in one headline, although the telehealth page states they are kept apart. Searchers and reviewers read titles first.

HTTPS with HSTS, one canonical host, no duplicate titles or descriptions, one H1 per page, no heading skips, no broken internal links, no noindex mistakes, hidden regulatory guides excluded from the sitemap and unlinked in HTML, image dimensions and CLS near zero on live, brotli compression, per-route OG images (verified 1200 by 630 JPEGs, 50 to 84 KB), manifest and icons present, `lang="en"` set, single language so no hreflang needed.
